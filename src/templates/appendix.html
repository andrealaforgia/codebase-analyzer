Normalization Methodology

Each analysis dimension produces a raw score that is normalized to a 0-10 scale. The overall health score is a weighted average of all available normalized dimension scores, scaled to 0-100.

Dimension Weight

Risk Model

Business risk assessments are derived from dimension scores:

Risk Category Contributing Dimensions
Maintainability RiskCode Quality, Cognitive Load, Refactoring Debt
Reliability RiskTest Design, Legacy Safety
Architecture RiskDDD Compliance, Code Quality

Scores generated by specialized AI analysis agents. When dimensions are missing, their weights are redistributed proportionally among the available dimensions.
